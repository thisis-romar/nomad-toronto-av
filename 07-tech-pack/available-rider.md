## DJ Equipment

### Media Players

**4× Pioneer CDJ-3000** Multi Player

- 9" touchscreen display
- USB / SD card / Pro DJ Link streaming
- 4-deck Pro DJ Link network (linked via DJM-V10)
- Rekordbox compatible (USB export recommended)
- Digital coax output per unit

### DJ Mixer

**Pioneer DJM-V10** 6-Channel DJ Mixer

- 6 channels (channels 1–4 available for guest use)
- Fader curve, crossfader assign fully adjustable
- Built-in Beat FX + Sound Color FX
- 3-band isolator per channel
- Master output: XLR balanced L/R to house matrix
- Booth output: XLR balanced L/R to house monitors

### House Matrix Mixer

**Allen & Heath CQ-12T** Digital Mixer (not typically artist-operated)

Managed by house technician. Handles routing to:
- Main PA (FOH)
- DJ booth monitors + sub
- Entrance fill speakers

## Available Inputs

| Input | Type | Location | Notes |
|-------|------|----------|-------|
| CH 1–2 | XLR or XLR/TRS combo | CQ-12T rear | Currently: DJM-V10 Master (house default) |
| CH 3–4 | XLR or XLR/TRS combo | CQ-12T rear | Currently: DJM-V10 Booth (house default) |
| **CH 5–10** | **XLR or XLR/TRS combo** | **CQ-12T rear** | **Available for guest inputs** |

> **For touring acts with their own mixer:** Connect your stereo master output to CQ-12T CH5+6 (XLR L/R). Advise production contact in advance.

> **For live acts:** Up to 6 additional mono inputs (CH5–10) available. XLR or TRS ¼". All channels have full HPF, gate, PEQ, compressor, and delay on input.

§6

Mismatches

Five of the eight profiles are patched to a footprint their fixture has no mode for. Ordered by what each actually does to the rig, not by profile number: only the first can corrupt another fixture's channels, and the last is merely untidy.

Can collide

Strobe bars — patched 13CH against a 4 / 16 / 168 fixture

If the bars are in 16CH they are three channels wider than their slot and five of the seven run into the bar addressed next — 396–398, 409–411, 422–424, 435–437, 448–450. Those are ch14–16 of the overrun bar (RGBW-effect speed, background colour, background dimmer) driven by ch1–3 of its neighbour (master dimmer, RGB strobe, red). If instead they are in 4CH, nothing collides but nine of every thirteen patched channels are dead and the console's dimmer, strobe and effects do nothing.

BAR 7 is absent from that list: it sits at 357 with the 370–382 gap after it, exactly the room a 16-channel bar needs. That gap was filed as an unexplained spare in June.

FixSet every bar to 16CH, re-patch on a 16-channel stride, load a matching profile.

Costs a function

Moving beams — patched 14CH against a 16 / 20 fixture

Nothing collides — the stride is already 16, so each beam occupies 280–295, 296–311, 312–327, 328–343 with no overlap and the next fixture at 357. But the 14-channel profile stops at pan and tilt speed, leaving ch15 Reset and ch16 Lamp control unreachable. On four discharge fixtures that means the lamps cannot be struck, doused or reset from the console.

Everything look-critical — colour, gobo, prism, frost, focus, pan, tilt and both fine channels — sits inside ch1–14 and works. The loss is confined to lamp handling.

FixLoad a 16-channel profile against the same start addresses. No re-addressing needed.

Lands on the wrong channel

DJ-deck bar — patched 11CH against a fixed 13

The MA profile allots a 2-channel master where the Microh has four (function select, then all-red / all-green / all-blue), so the nine segment channels are offset by two: the console's cell-1 red drives the fixture's all-blue, and so on down the bar.

Nothing collides. The bar would occupy 11–23 and the next fixture is a laser bar at 27, so the 22–26 gap absorbs the two extra channels.

FixLoad a 13-channel profile at the same start address.

Half a fixture

Hazer — patched 1CH against a fixed 2CH personality

The Hurricane Haze 2D's own DMX is two channels: blower speed, then haze volume. If what is patched is the hazer's DMX, haze volume never responds to the console. If it is a DP-415 pack channel instead, the patch is correct as written and the hazer runs on its own front-panel settings.

Which of the two it is decides whether this is a fault or a non-issue, and it is one look at the back of the hazer.

FixEstablish first whether the hazer's DMX is connected at all. Only then decide.

Wastes addresses

Laser bars — patched 26CH against an 11 / 19 / 24 fixture

Because 24 < 26 nothing collides; each bar simply leaves two dead channels, eighteen across the nine bars.

What cannot be checked from the export is the profile's internal channel order — grandMA2 writes fixture-type names into an XML fixture list, not their definitions. If the profile replicates the LS650's 24CH map with two pad channels everything works; if it is a different 26-channel bar, every attribute lands wrong, which would be obvious on sight.

FixConfirm the mode reads 24CH, then load a 24-channel profile and reclaim the two channels.

§7

Inverted fixtures

Two of the eight profiles are pan/tilt-inverted duplicates of another profile, for fixtures hung upside down. Reported by the venue and corroborated against the export.

Inverted profileDuplicate ofFixturesCorroboration
6 movingwash zone5 NEW WASH M.Wash 7, M.Wash 10 Identical sub-fixture structure — one cell of 9 channels. A zoned mode would have cells.
7 LASER BARS - Invert 26CH8 LASER BARS 26CH Laser.BAR(6) 1 Named “Invert”, and its six cell addresses run descending (215→210) where every other bar ascends.

Pan/tilt inversion lives in the fixture-type definition, which grandMA2 does not write into an XML fixture list — so it cannot be read out of the export directly. What the export can show is that each duplicate has the same sub-fixture shape as its base, meaning the two differ only in something the file cannot represent. That is corroboration, not proof.

Three fixtures are therefore hung inverted — M.Wash 7, M.Wash 10 and Laser.BAR(6) 1. This is the only fixture-orientation information the repo holds. Worth raising with whoever maintains the showfile: grandMA2 inverts pan and tilt per fixture in the patch, so carrying a duplicate fixture type works but splits each group across two types and grows the profile count with every orientation.

4.44 A known. Whatever the CO₂ jets draw is bounded by the pack whether or not anyone measures it: at most 5 A each by channel rating, at most 10.6 A between them once the hazer is accounted for. A jet is a solenoid valve, so the real figure should sit far below that.

Dip switch 10 must read Switch

The Hurricane Haze 2D's manual states outright that it must not be run on a dimmer. The DP-415 selects Dimmer or Switch pack-wide on dip switch 10, not per channel. The hazer shares that pack with both CO₂ jets, so it cannot be moved to a switch-mode pack of its own.

There is therefore exactly one correct setting, and it has not been read.

§9

Laser safety

Each LS650 carries six 500 mW emitters at 638 nm — 3 W of optical output per bar, nine bars. At 500 mW per aperture these are Class 4 by any measure: the class where the beam is an eye and skin hazard including from diffuse reflections, and where audience scanning stops being a design choice and becomes a regulated one.

§10

Open items

What is not known, and what would settle it. Everything above the fold here is readable off a fixture panel in a single walk round the rig.

  • DP-415 dip switch 10The Hurricane Haze 2D must not be run on a dimmer, the pack's Dimmer/Switch selection is pack-wide, and the hazer shares the pack with both CO₂ jets — so it cannot be isolated. It must read Switch. The only question in this document with a single correct answer.
  • Strobe-bar DMX mode13CH is impossible, so whatever the panel reads is new information. CH16 means five bars are colliding; CH04 means nine of thirteen channels per bar are dead.
  • Beam model plateConfirms the YF BEAM 230 against the load schedule. Four discharge beams are 1400–1600 W where four LED mini heads would have been 400 W — a 1.2 kW swing in the total.
  • CO₂ jet make, model and wattageThe last load with no figure. Bounded by the pack at ≤5 A each and ≤10.6 A between them once the hazer's 4.44 A is accounted for, but unmeasured.
  • Hazer DMX — connected or notDecides whether the 1CH patch is a correct pack channel or a truncated 2CH personality.
  • DP-415 start address and socket assignmentGives the CO₂ jets and the hazer their real addresses. They are pack channels, so the long-standing “CO₂ unpatched at address 0” item was asking the wrong question.
  • Inverted hang — M.Wash 7, M.Wash 10, Laser.BAR(6) 1Confirms the duplicate-profile reading and is the only fixture-orientation data the repo holds. Every AbsolutePosition in the showfile is 0,0,0.
  • Laser class labelConfirms the Class 4 declaration on the fixture body.
  • Fixture positionsNo to-scale plot can be drawn without a survey. This also blocks any assessment of whether a laser bar can reach audience-accessible space.
  • DMX node make, model and universe/port mappingThe showfile patches addresses but records nothing about what physically drives U1 and U2.
  • Lighting mains feed, breakers and power factorPer-fixture draw is known; the feed that carries it is not. The currents in §8 assume unity power factor and are a floor, not a design figure.
  • Rev 7.2's repo link points at <code>main</code>The Rev 7.2 sources page cites 08-lighting/nomad-lighting-spec-v1.md on main, which is 22 commits behind this work and still carries the retracted Clay Paky Sharpy attribution. Anyone following that link from the PDF lands on a claim the PDF itself supersedes. Merge the branch or repoint the link.
  • Console hardware and booth position“Nomad” is the dongle-licensed software. Whether the surface on site is onPC with a dongle, a command wing or a full console is not in the export.

The evidence

What actually made each link

The showfile records a profile name and a start address, and nothing else — no fixture-type definitions, no makes, no models. So each identification rests on a specific fact. These are those facts, strongest first.

YF BEAM 230

Confirmed by manual
  • 9 Sharpy Standard Lamp on ×4

The four beams are patched on a uniform 16-channel stride — exactly this fixture's 16CH mode, and two wider than the 14CH profile loaded against them. The manual confirms 16/20CH and the 189 W 5R Philips / 230 W 7R Osram lamp.

“Standard Lamp on” is a Clay Paky Sharpy mode name, and a Sharpy's own lamp is a 189 W MSD Platinum 5R — so the profile was a sensible pick for a Sharpy-clone. The brand was never right; the class was.

Panda LS650 / LS652

Near-certain
  • 8 LASER BARS 26CH ×8
  • 7 LASER BARS - Invert 26CH ×1

Six laser eyes against the MA fixture name Laser.BAR(6) and its six sub-fixture cells.

The structure matches attribute for attribute: MA records one master of 7 attributes plus 6 cells of 2. The LS650's 24CH mode gives each eye exactly two — a tilt position and a laser on/off — with seven whole-fixture attributes. 7 + 6×2 = 19, which is what the export holds.

Elation DP-415 — 4-channel dimmer/switch pack

Confirmed by the venue

The three 2 Dimmer 00 entries are not fixtures. They are channels on this pack — mains switching for the hazer and both CO₂ jets, three of its four channels. 120 V, 15 A total, 5 A per channel.

The disagreements

Five mismatches, five different costs

Ordered by what each one actually does to the rig, not by profile number. Only the first can corrupt another fixture's channels; the last is merely untidy.

Can collide

Strobe bars — patched 13CH, fixture offers 4 / 16 / 168

If the bars are in 16CH they are three channels wider than their slot, and five of the seven run into the bar addressed next: 396–398, 409–411, 422–424, 435–437, 448–450. Those are ch14–16 of the overrun bar — RGBW-effect speed, background colour, background dimmer — driven by ch1–3 of its neighbour. If instead they are in 4CH, nothing collides but nine of every thirteen patched channels are dead.

BAR 7 is not in that list. It sits at 357 with the 370–382 gap after it — exactly the room a 16-channel bar needs. That gap was filed as an unexplained spare in June.

Costs a function

Moving beams — patched 14CH, fixture offers 16 / 20

Nothing collides: the stride is already 16. But the 14-channel profile stops at Pan and Tilt speed, so ch15 Reset and ch16 Lamp control are unreachable. On four discharge fixtures that means the lamps cannot be struck, doused or reset from the console.

Everything look-critical — colour, gobo, prism, frost, focus, pan, tilt, both fine channels — sits inside 1–14 and works. The loss is entirely in lamp handling.

Lands on the wrong channel

DJ-deck bar — patched 11CH, fixture is a fixed 13

The profile's master is 2 channels where the Microh has 4, so the whole segment block is offset by two: the console's cell-1 red drives the fixture's all-blue, and so on down the bar.

Nothing collides — the bar occupies 11–23 and the next fixture is a laser at 27.

Half a fixture

Hazer — patched 1CH, personality is a fixed 2

The Hurricane Haze 2D's own DMX is two channels: blower speed, then haze volume. If what is patched is the hazer's DMX, haze volume never responds. If it is a DP-415 pack channel instead, the patch is correct as written.

Which of the two it is decides whether this is a fault or a non-issue. It is one look at the back of the hazer.

Wastes addresses

Laser bars — patched 26CH, fixture offers 11 / 19 / 24

Because 24 < 26 nothing collides; each bar simply leaves two dead channels, eighteen across the nine bars.

What cannot be checked from here is the profile's internal channel order — the export carries fixture-type names, not their definitions.

Still unread

What a walk round the rig would settle

Every one of these fixtures shows its mode and address on its own panel. Nothing here should be re-patched from a desk — the patch is internally consistent as it stands.

  • Must readDP-415 dip switch 10. The Hurricane Haze 2D must not be run on a dimmer, the pack's mode is pack-wide, and the hazer shares that pack with both CO₂ jets — so it cannot be isolated. It must read Switch. The only question here with a single correct answer.
  • Highest valueOne strobe bar's DMX mode. 13CH is impossible, so whatever the panel says is news. CH16 means five bars are colliding.
  • Worth 1.2 kWA beam's model plate. Confirms the YF BEAM 230 against the load schedule — four discharge beams are 1400–1600 W where four LED mini heads would have been 400 W.
  • Last unknownThe CO₂ jets' wattage. Bounded by the pack at ≤5 A each and ≤10.6 A between them, but nobody has read the real figure.
  • RiggingM.Wash 7, M.Wash 10, Laser.BAR 1 — hung inverted? Confirms the duplicate-profile reading, and is the only fixture-orientation data the repo holds. Every position in the showfile is 0,0,0.
Load

5.73–5.93 kW · 48–49 A at 120 V

A subtotal, not a total: only the CO₂ jets are missing a figure. Currents are real power ÷ voltage at unity power factor, so they are a floor — the fixtures' actual power factor and the discharge ballasts' inrush go on top before anything is sized. Nine laser bars at 6 × 500 mW / 638 nm each make this a Class 4 installation, which is a regulated question rather than a lighting-design one.

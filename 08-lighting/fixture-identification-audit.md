# Lighting Fixture Identification Audit

**Trigger:** the venue supplied three fixture manuals, now committed to `08-lighting/manuals/`.
Until now every fixture in the rig was a **generic MA profile name with no known real-world model**
(`nomad-lighting-spec-v1.md` §5, Open Item §9.3). These manuals close part of that gap and open a
new question that matters more.

**Method.** The showfile records a fixture-profile *name* and a start address per fixture. It does
**not** contain the fixture-type definitions, so a profile's internal channel order cannot be read
back from it — only its footprint, from the `…CH` in the name, corroborated by the address spacing
between consecutive fixtures of the same type. What *can* be checked mechanically is whether that
footprint is a mode the real fixture can actually be switched to, and what happens if it isn't.
Everything below is generated by `scripts/audit-dmx-patch.py`.

## §4 Finding 1 — the strobe bars: 13CH is not a mode

**The seven `LED.Strobe-BAR` fixtures are patched 13 channels apart. The Light4Me STROBE MULTI BAR
can only be set to 4, 16 or 168.** One of three things is true, and they have different symptoms:

**(a) The bars are in 16CH mode.** Each bar is then 3 channels wider than its patch slot and runs
into the bar addressed after it:

<!-- generated: python3 scripts/audit-dmx-patch.py --md -->

| Fixture | Occupies | Runs into | Shared addresses |
|---------|----------|-----------|------------------|
| LED.Strobe-BAR 6 | 383–398 | LED.Strobe-BAR 5 (starts 396) | **396–398** (3 ch) |
| LED.Strobe-BAR 5 | 396–411 | LED.Strobe-BAR 4 (starts 409) | **409–411** (3 ch) |
| LED.Strobe-BAR 4 | 409–424 | LED.Strobe-BAR 3 (starts 422) | **422–424** (3 ch) |
| LED.Strobe-BAR 3 | 422–437 | LED.Strobe-BAR 2 (starts 435) | **435–437** (3 ch) |
| LED.Strobe-BAR 2 | 435–450 | LED.Strobe-BAR 1 (starts 448) | **448–450** (3 ch) |

The shared addresses are CH14–16 of the overrun bar (RGBW-effect speed, background colour,
background dimmer) driven by CH1–3 of the next one (master dimmer, RGB strobe, red). **Symptom:
the background colour and background dimmer of five bars follow the neighbouring bar's intensity
instead of the console.** Note BAR 7 is not in the list — it sits at 357 with the 370–382 gap after
it, which is exactly the room a 16-channel bar needs. That gap was previously flagged as an
unexplained spare (`nomad-lighting-spec-v1.md` §9.5); 16CH mode would explain it.

**(b) The bars are in 4CH mode.** No collision, but only R/G/B/W respond — the console's master
dimmer, strobe and all effect attributes do nothing, and 9 of every 13 patched channels are dead.

**(c) They are not Light4Me bars.** Then some other 13CH RGBW bar is in the rig and this manual
belongs to something else.

Cases (a) and (b) both mean the MA profile does not describe the fixture, and the fix is the same:
set every bar to 16CH, re-patch on a 16-channel stride, and load a matching profile.

## §5 Finding 2 — the laser bars: 26CH is not a mode either

**The nine `Laser.BAR(6)` fixtures are patched on a 26-channel stride. The LS650 offers 11, 19
and 24.** This one does **not** collide — 24 < 26, so each bar leaves 2 dead channels and the next
bar starts clear. 18 channels are wasted across the nine bars; nothing breaks.

What cannot be checked from here is whether the profile's channel *order* matches. The showfile
carries no fixture-type definitions, so the 26-channel layout inside `8 LASER BARS 26CH` is
invisible to this audit. Two possibilities:

- The profile replicates the LS650's 24CH map with 2 pad channels → everything works, and the only
  cost is the wasted addresses.
- The profile is a different 26-channel laser bar → **every attribute lands on the wrong channel**,
  and the bars would be visibly uncontrollable rather than subtly wrong. That is easy to rule out
  by eye and presumably already has been.

There is circumstantial support for the first: the MA sub-fixture layout for each bar is **one
master plus six cells of two attributes each**, and the LS650's 24CH map gives each eye exactly two
attributes — a tilt position (CH4–9) and a laser on/off (CH14–19) — with seven whole-fixture
attributes (pan, pan fine, pan speed, all-tilt, tilt speed, master switch, strobe). 7 + 6×2 = 19
patched attributes, which is what the showfile records. The remaining 5 channels of the 24CH mode
(effect select, effect speed, motor auto-run, walk speed, reset) are simply not exposed as MA
attributes.

> `Laser.BAR(6) 1` uses the **invert** profile and its six cell addresses descend (215→210) rather
> than ascend. That is consistent with one bar hung the other way round, and is unaffected by this
> finding.

## §6 What to check on site — 10 minutes, no tools

Every one of these fixtures shows its mode and address on its own LCD. Read them off; do not
change anything until all three are recorded.

| # | Fixture | Read from the panel | Expect if the patch is right |
|---|---------|---------------------|------------------------------|
| 1 | Any one strobe bar | DMX mode (`CH04` / `CH16` / `CH168`) and address `A001–A512` | **Nothing** — 13CH is impossible. Record which it actually is. |
| 2 | All seven strobe bars | Address on each | 357, 383, 396, 409, 422, 435, 448 |
| 3 | Any one laser bar | Channel Mode (`11CH` / `19CH` / `24CH`) and DMX address | Expect **24CH**; addresses 27, 53, 79, 105, 131, 157, 183, 209, and 513 on U2 |
| 4 | One moving wash and one moving beam | Channel Mode setting | Wash **9CH**, beam **14CH** — and confirm both are the same physical fixture |
| 5 | Moving beams | Physical model plate | Confirms or refutes BETOPPER LM70S; settles the Sharpy question outright |
| 6 | DJ-deck LED bar | Make/model plate, mode, address | Address 11, 11CH. No manual on file. |
| 7 | CO₂ jets and hazer | Make/model, DMX address, trigger method | Jets are **unpatched** (address 0) — see spec §9.1 |
